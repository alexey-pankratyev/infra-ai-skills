# Decision Analysis

Turn ambiguous choices into explicit decisions.

Define:
- Objective
- Constraints
- Decision criteria
- Alternatives
- Risks
- Reversibility
- Opportunity cost

Use qualitative or quantitative scoring only when it adds value. Avoid fake precision.

Conclude with:
recommended option → why → what could change the decision → immediate next step.
